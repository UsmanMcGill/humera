# Step-by-step plan: preparation to the first 90 days in Cork

Prepared 11 October 2026. Read [the knowledge base](README.md) for linked rules and role requirements. Dates below are planning targets, not application deadlines or guaranteed processing times. Shift the arrival-relative weeks if travel changes.

## The working strategy

Run two tracks together: suitable paid office/education support applications, and evidence collection for teacher recognition. Add SNA applications only after agreeing acceptable care duties. Do not wait for teacher recognition to apply for office roles.

Suggested weekly effort before arrival: 4 to 6 hours of preparation. After arrival: 6 to 8 hours of job-search work, adjusted around settling in. These are proposed routines, not additional employment obligations.

## Stage 1: next seven days, 11 to 18 October 2026

### Step 1. Create the factual record

- [ ] Write her full professional name exactly as on certificates.
- [ ] List each qualification, institution, programme length and graduation date.
- [ ] List employers, precise start/end dates, job titles and subjects.
- [ ] Confirm the last teaching date and explain any subsequent period accurately.
- [ ] List real duties, achievements and research methods. Use [the application kit](APPLICATION_KIT.md).
- [ ] Confirm morning availability and what occasional longer days would mean.
- [ ] Discuss SNA personal care, mobility and behavioural support separately. Record what she is willing to do and what training/support she needs.

**Done when:** there is a reliable chronology and a one-paragraph availability statement. Unknowns are marked, not filled with guesses.

### Step 2. Request documents before leaving Pakistan

- [ ] Degree certificates and transcripts for BSc, M.Phil. and B.Ed.
- [ ] Module descriptors for science and education courses.
- [ ] Official B.Ed. supervised-placement details.
- [ ] Letters confirming instruction AND examination in English, where true.
- [ ] Employment letters from school/college and agreement from two referees.
- [ ] Clarification of teacher recognition/induction evidence from the relevant Pakistani authority.
- [ ] Identify the police-clearance process and check the receiving body's format/recency requirements before ordering.

**Done when:** each request has an owner, request date and follow-up date. Keep scans and originals securely outside the repository.

## Stage 2: by 31 October 2026

### Step 3. Start recognition research

Follow [the detailed degree recognition tutorial](DEGREE_RECOGNITION.md) for the document checklist, QQI/NARIC searches and Teaching Council application steps.

Search QQI/NARIC for the precise qualifications and save matching advisory statements. Request advice if the award is absent. Build a Biology module table covering credits/contact hours, level, subject areas and practical work. Do not claim equivalence before assessment.

Draft a concise Teaching Council enquiry using the kit. Ask about the correct overseas route, professional-status evidence, induction, the timebound provision, English evidence and fees. An enquiry is not a pre-approval. Submit a formal application when the evidence and route are sufficiently clear.

**Done when:** recognition next steps and missing evidence are recorded. [QQI](https://www.qqi.ie/recognition-of-foreign-qualifications) and [Teaching Council overseas route](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/i-am-applying-to-register/qualified-outside-of-ireland/register-as-a-post-primary-teacher/).

### Step 4. Build two CV versions

Create a two-page administration/support CV and an education/SNA CV. The administration version leads with organisation, records, communication and software she actually uses. The education version leads with biology teaching and pupil support. State the expected move and accurate permission status.

Prepare a basic portfolio with fictional data: an Excel attendance table, a meeting agenda, and a short biology lesson outline. It must contain no pupil information and make no claim to previous Irish employment.

**Done when:** both CVs are fact-checked and ready to tailor, and the portfolio demonstrates current practical skills.

## Stage 3: November 2026

### Step 5. Improve the one clear skills gap

Practise Excel for 20 to 30 minutes, four days weekly: sorting/filtering, tables, SUM, AVERAGE, COUNTIF, dates and a simple chart. Add lookups/pivots only after the basics are comfortable. Make one clean spreadsheet from fictional records without step-by-step help.

Complete Tusla's Children First introduction if pursuing school roles. Keep the certificate. [Tusla programme](https://www.tusla.ie/children-first/children-first-e-learning-programme/).

### Step 6. Set up the search

- [ ] Create her own job-search email and job-board accounts.
- [ ] Save alerts on UCC, MTU, Cork ETB, EducationPosts and paid JobsIreland listings where alerts are available.
- [ ] Record ten plausible employer targets near UCC or on workable transport routes.
- [ ] Review ten relevant adverts to identify repeated essential skills and timetable patterns.
- [ ] Ask course providers about the next suitable SNA intake, prerequisites and all-in fees.

**Done when:** alerts are useful, the employer list is relevant, and course decisions are informed by actual job requirements.

## Stage 4: December 2026 to travel

### Step 7. Start selective applications

Apply to vacancies whose start dates could accommodate late-January arrival or later. Submit roughly 3 to 5 well-matched applications per week if that many exist. Do not send low-fit applications merely to meet a target. Use online interviews where the employer permits them.

Check essential qualifications, immigration eligibility, hours, location and deadline before writing. Follow required forms rather than sending a CV where a portal is mandatory. Use one polite follow-up after the employer's stated timeline, if its instructions allow.

### Step 8. Resolve immigration and travel logistics

Ask UCC/ISD to confirm Humera's expected permission, exact right-to-work evidence after arrival, registration documents and whether the sponsor must attend. Reconcile the husband's 11 January start with the family's late-January travel so her CV does not show an incorrect availability date.

Budget immigration fees, insurance, translations and Dublin travel separately. Check registration booking guidance again; current guidance permits first-time appointment booking after arrival. [ISD checklist](https://www.irishimmigration.ie/registering-your-immigration-permission/how-to-register-your-immigration-permission-for-the-first-time/required-documents/).

**Done when:** she knows what evidence an employer will receive and what remains pending. No job start is promised on an assumed permission.

## Stage 5: arrival week and weeks 1 to 2, late January/February 2027

### Step 9. Complete the essentials while continuing applications

- [ ] Obtain an address/contact number and update applications.
- [ ] Book/attend immigration registration through the official process and check her actual work conditions.
- [ ] Apply for PPS when there is a valid reason; searching alone is not sufficient.
- [ ] Attend the required PPS appointment and monitor the application.
- [ ] Arrange a bank/payment account acceptable to payroll.
- [ ] Test the commute at the actual work time, including return travel.
- [ ] Respond promptly to employer vetting and reference requests.

[MyWelfare PPS process](https://services.mywelfare.ie/en/topics/identity-services/personal-public-service-pps-number/). Do not stop searching while waiting for PPS; clarify payroll onboarding with the employer.

## Stage 6: weeks 3 to 6

### Step 10. Run a sustainable weekly routine

| Day | Task |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | Review alerts and choose suitable vacancies |
| Tuesday | Tailor one or two applications |
| Wednesday | Practise interview examples and a practical skill |
| Thursday | Finish applications and permitted follow-ups |
| Friday | Update tracker, recognition progress and next week's priorities |

Aim for 4 to 6 strong applications weekly when vacancies support it. Give most attention to administration/school offices, some to suitable academic/research support, and SNA roles whose duties she accepts. Do not apply to regulated teaching posts claiming registration she does not hold.

### Step 11. Diagnose the results

- No interviews after 15 well-matched applications: review CV evidence and whether essential criteria/hours actually matched.
- Interviews but no offers after several attempts: practise specific examples, software tasks and clear availability answers.
- Few compatible vacancies: broaden job titles, consider temporary employment, or accept 09:00 to 14:00 if suitable. Discuss any further change in hours/commute first.
- Recognition delayed: follow official missing-document requests while keeping office applications active.
- SNA duties unsuitable: focus on school administration and academic support; do not buy more SNA training to solve a duties mismatch.

These are review triggers, not forecasts of success or failure.

## Stage 7: weeks 7 to 12 and first job

### Step 12. Accept a suitable offer carefully

Use the offer checklist in the kit. Confirm employee status, hours, finish time, paid breaks, duties, pay, term-time arrangements, contract duration and onboarding conditions. Register the first Irish employment with Revenue and check the first payslip. [Revenue instructions](https://www.revenue.ie/en/jobs-and-pensions/starting-your-first-job/index.aspx).

After two weeks, review actual workload, commute and finish times with her. After one month, assess whether the schedule works for the family. Continue teacher recognition only where the required time/cost is feasible and she still wants that route.

## If she is still searching after 90 days

Review actual evidence before changing direction. Keep the original morning/commute preferences unless she agrees to change them. Seek feedback from a legitimate employment adviser or recruiter. Consider a single targeted course under the agreed budget if multiple suitable adverts request it. Plan for the next school-year recruitment cycle while continuing paid office applications. Do not treat a lack of immediate offers as proof that her Pakistani qualifications or experience have no value.
