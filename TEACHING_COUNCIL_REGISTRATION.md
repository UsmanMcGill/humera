# Teaching Council registration and using QQI/NARIC statements

Prepared 11 October 2026. Recheck the official requirements before applying in 2027. This tutorial explains how to prepare and apply; it is not an eligibility decision.

Read alongside [degree recognition](DEGREE_RECOGNITION.md), [the application kit](APPLICATION_KIT.md) and [the action plan](ACTION_PLAN.md).

## 1. What each process does

| Process | Purpose | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| QQI/NARIC academic comparability | Advice comparing an overseas qualification with an Irish award type and NFQ level | Teacher registration, English proficiency, permission to work or a guaranteed job |
| Teaching Council registration | Professional assessment and registration for an applicable teaching route | A job offer or an agreed morning timetable |
| Employer or course admission assessment | Decides whether the evidence meets a particular vacancy or course requirement | Automatic acceptance elsewhere |

The document commonly called a “QQI equivalency certificate” is normally a **QQI/NARIC comparability statement**. It is free, generic and advisory, rather than a personalised Irish degree or a legal recognition certificate. Employers, admissions teams and professional authorities make their own decisions. [QQI explanation](https://www.qqi.ie/recognition-of-foreign-qualifications)

Keep the Pakistani award's actual title, institution and year in every application. A comparison does not rename or replace the qualification.

## 2. Humera's starting position

The family reports a BSc honours in Zoology, an M.Phil. in Zoology from the University of Sargodha, and a one-year AIOU B.Ed. completed in 2013–2014 through Urdu. Her existing school employment reportedly meant separate teaching supervision was not required. Certificates, transcripts and an official exemption or assessment record have not been inspected.

**The first route to investigate is overseas post-primary registration for Biology.** This follows her high-school and F.Sc. teaching background, but does not establish eligibility. Do not describe F.Sc. teaching as university lecturing.

There are three questions to resolve:

1. Which QQI profile applies to the historical B.Ed.?
2. What practical teaching was formally assessed or exempted within that programme?
3. Do her science qualifications satisfy the Council's Biology criteria?

### The B.Ed. comparison correction

[Entry 616](https://qsearch.qqi.ie/WebPart/RecognitionDetails?recognitionCode=616) specifically describes Pakistan's one-year Bachelor of Education as a conversion/professional training programme, comparable to a **Higher Certificate at NFQ Level 6**. Its profile describes admission after a BA, BCom or BSc Pass degree and secondary teaching up to grade 10.

[Entry 152](https://qsearch.qqi.ie/WebPart/RecognitionDetails?recognitionCode=152) is a broader postgraduate bachelor category, compared to NFQ Level 8, whose description also mentions a one-year B.Ed. These descriptions overlap. **Do not claim Level 8 for Humera's B.Ed. on the strength of entry 152.** Entry 616 is the closer reference for planning; ask QQI about her exact award and admission route if the match remains uncertain.

Neither comparison is an individual Teaching Council decision. A lower comparison does not itself prove refusal, and a higher comparison would not prove professional eligibility.

## 3. Before starting the Teaching Council application

### Step 1: make a private document folder

Suggested folders: Qualifications, BEdPractice, ScienceModules, ProfessionalStatus, EnglishEvidence, Vetting, SubmittedApplication and Decisions.

Keep passports, certificates, transcripts, addresses, police records and referee details outside this public repository. Use clear filenames and preserve originals.

### Step 2: collect the academic documents

For each degree request:

- Certificate and complete official transcript, including grading explanations.
- Start/completion dates, normal duration and admission qualification.
- Official module descriptions for the actual study years.
- Credit/contact-hour explanations where available.
- Official translations for required material written in Urdu.

For science studies add laboratory details and the M.Phil. thesis information. Do not invent ECTS conversions.

### Step 3: obtain AIOU's practical-training evidence

Ask AIOU to clarify the **2013–2014 one-year programme**, rather than supplying today's 1.5-year syllabus.

Request a letter stating:

- The practical teaching requirement for her cohort.
- Whether employed teachers could practise in their own school.
- Whether her existing classroom work was observed, supervised, assessed or credited.
- The dates, hours/weeks, pupil ages, assessor and result, where recorded.
- Whether she received an exemption, its formal basis and the evidence accepted.

If there was no practical assessment, state that accurately. Do not describe ordinary employment as supervised placement. Experience letters are useful supporting evidence, but do not automatically replace assessed training.

The Council can identify placement shortfalls, but conditional registration is not available in every case. Its policy includes refusal circumstances involving incomplete qualifications, lack of prior teacher recognition or an obvious qualification shortfall. [Qualification shortfalls policy](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/i-am-a-registered-teacher/registration-with-conditions/qualification-shortfalls-qs/)

### Step 4: clarify professional status and induction

The overseas route requires evidence of full professional qualification from the relevant competent authority. Ask the Council which Pakistani authority and documents it requires. A university award, an employment letter and formal professional recognition serve different purposes.

Also distinguish initial B.Ed. practice from **post-qualification induction/probation**. If induction is missing, investigate the current timebound provision. It addresses eligible applicants' missing post-qualification induction, not a blanket waiver of initial teacher training. [Overseas registration](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/i-am-applying-to-register/qualified-outside-of-ireland/register-as-a-post-primary-teacher/) and [2025 registration regulations](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/2025/si/482/made/en/)

Seek procedural guidance before paying for courses. Do not expect an informal enquiry to provide a binding pre-approval.

## 4. Prepare the Biology evidence

Open the [current subject requirements](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/assets/uploads/2026/01/05.01.26-Curricular-Subject-Requirements.pdf) and find Biology. Recheck the version linked from the Council website on the application date.

The published Biology criteria include degree level, overall and Biology credits or equivalents, study through third-year level or higher, breadth across at least four specified biological areas and laboratory work. The listed thresholds are NFQ Level 8 or equivalent, 180 ECTS equivalent overall and 60 in Biology.

Use a table to help the assessor locate evidence:

| Actual module and qualification | Official credits/hours and study level | Relevant Biology area | Practical component | Evidence page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fill from records | Fill from records | For example, ecology or genetics | Recorded lab work | Transcript/descriptor page |

Map all relevant studies, but let the Council decide what counts. An M.Phil. in Zoology does not automatically establish every area needed to teach school Biology.

## 5. Address English and translations separately

**An Urdu-taught B.Ed. does not by itself require repeating the degree in English.** She needs accepted English evidence.

First check whether her BSc was **both taught and examined in English**. If so, request a university letter confirming both facts. The Council accepts an undergraduate or teacher education qualification through English, with an institutional confirmation where English is not the country's vernacular.

Published test alternatives include IELTS Academic at 7.0 or above or Cambridge C1 Advanced at Grade C or above. All components must be taken in the same sitting. The normal test-age limit is five years, subject to the Council's stated exception. Verify the live conditions before booking. Do not assume conversational fluency or an English-language thesis alone is sufficient. [English requirements](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/i-am-applying-to-register/english-language-requirement/)

Translation concerns the **documents' language**, not the course's medium. English-issued records do not need translation merely because the programme was taught in Urdu. Required Urdu records need certified English or Irish translations. Keep each translation with the original. Follow the Council's approved verification arrangements. [Translation guidance](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/i-am-applying-to-register/qualified-outside-of-ireland/register-as-a-post-primary-teacher/)

## 6. Character and vetting preparation

Use the current character/reference forms and answer declarations accurately. Gather a complete address history and check the required referees against the live instructions.

Irish Garda vetting and overseas police clearance are separate. The Council administers teacher vetting. Follow the invitation process and its accepted ID/address policy rather than buying an independent “vetting certificate.”

The Council's published overview requires police clearance for relevant countries where overseas residence totals 12 months or more after age 18, with an exception for already registered teachers. Check the detailed policy for country coverage and timing before ordering records. [Vetting overview](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/vetting/) and [overseas clearance](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/vetting/overseas-police-clearance/)

A Pakistani police certificate does not replace Garda vetting. Send sensitive records through the required official channel, not through GitHub.

## 7. Submit through My Registration

The exact screen labels can change. This is a preparation sequence, not a claim that a live application has been completed.

1. Open the [overseas post-primary route page](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/i-am-applying-to-register/qualified-outside-of-ireland/register-as-a-post-primary-teacher/).
2. Follow its **My Registration** link. Use her own email and create an applicant account. Use her actual current address.
3. Open **Applications** and select the appropriate overseas route.
4. Read the live checklist and [post-primary application guidelines](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/assets/uploads/2024/01/Teaching-Council-Post-Primary-Guidelines-1.pdf). Older PDFs must be read with the current website.
5. Enter qualification and teaching-service details from records. Keep school placement separate from employment.
6. Upload the requested evidence, including translations, professional status and relevant science information.
7. Complete the English, character, vetting and declarations requirements as directed.
8. Review the complete form against the original documents. Explain missing evidence honestly.
9. Check the applicable fee, pay through the official portal and submit.
10. Save the acknowledgement, reference, payment receipt and submitted pack privately.

Set a reminder before the published three-month draft expiry. The Council estimates up to 12 weeks after receiving all required documents. Allow extra time for obtaining records. Payment or an acknowledgement does not mean registration is approved. [Current application timing](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/i-am-applying-to-register/qualified-outside-of-ireland/register-as-a-post-primary-teacher/)

Do not assume a QQI statement is a compulsory substitute for transcripts in this portal. Include it where requested or permitted as supporting academic advice. The Council's evidence checklist controls.

## 8. Respond to the decision

| Outcome | Next action |
| --- | --- |
| Additional evidence requested | List each missing item, obtain it from the appropriate body and reply with labelled files |
| Conditional registration | Record each condition, deadline and approved completion method; confirm any course before enrolling |
| Refusal or disputed condition | Read reasons and review instructions promptly; published guidance gives a 21-day review window |
| Registration finalised | Retain the registration details and renewal date; state the actual route, subject and conditions in applications |

Overseas post-primary applicants receive the History and Structure of the Irish Education System condition. Other shortfalls may require approved study or adaptation, normally within three years. Her own decision letter is essential. [Council decisions and conditions](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/i-am-applying-to-register/qualified-outside-of-ireland/register-as-a-post-primary-teacher/)

Registration with conditions is not the same as an unrestricted outcome. Neither outcome guarantees a part-time morning timetable.

## 9. Download the correct QQI statement

Use this process independently for the BSc, M.Phil. and B.Ed.:

1. Open the [official foreign qualifications database](https://qsearch.qqi.ie/WebPart/Search?searchtype=recognitions).
2. Select Pakistan and search for the qualification.
3. Compare title, duration, admission requirements and award profile against her records.
4. Open a matching profile's **Comparability Statement** PDF and save it.
5. Record the entry number, URL and download date.
6. If the profile is ambiguous, request advice through the link on [QQI's recognition page](https://www.qqi.ie/recognition-of-foreign-qualifications).

For her B.Ed., start with entry 616 and explain the overlap with entry 152 in an enquiry. For her BSc and M.Phil., use the separate checks in [degree recognition](DEGREE_RECOGNITION.md).

The downloadable statement is not personalised proof that QQI has authenticated her documents. Keep the actual certificate and transcript available for the receiving organisation.

## 10. Where to use QQI comparability statements

These are practical ways to present supporting evidence, not promises that the recipient must accept it.

| Situation | How to use it | Who decides |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Office, school administration or university support application | Attach or link the applicable statement when qualifications/equivalence evidence is requested | Employer/recruitment panel |
| Research or academic support vacancy | Supply the relevant science award statement with the certificate and requested research evidence | Hiring institution |
| Course admission | Ask admissions whether the statement supports entry and what additional documents are needed | Education provider |
| SNA vacancy | Offer applicable academic evidence if asked about overseas qualifications; ask about the specific advertised standard | School/employer |
| Teaching Council application | Supply as supporting advice where requested/permitted, alongside the professional evidence | Teaching Council |
| CV or cover letter | Add a brief comparison only after the profile's fit has been checked | Recipient assesses its relevance |

A B.Ed. comparison does not establish an SNA qualification, a special education teaching qualification or eligibility for every Level 6 vacancy. Subject, award type, skills and the actual advertisement still matter.

### Example: an employer requests “Level 8 or equivalent”

Check the qualification that supports that requirement. A verified Level 8 comparison for one award does not apply to every other award. Do not use the Level 6 B.Ed. profile to claim Level 8.

If the appropriate profile has been established, a cover-letter sentence could be:

> I hold [exact overseas award] from [institution]. The applicable QQI/NARIC profile compares this award type to [Irish award class], NFQ Level [number]. I can provide the statement and my original qualification documents.

Fill placeholders from verified evidence. Until the B.Ed. profile is resolved, list the actual B.Ed. without an asserted NFQ level.

For teacher applications, use “Teaching Council application submitted, decision pending” only after submission. Use “registered” only once registration is finalised, stating conditions accurately.

## 12. Suggested working sequence

This is a work schedule, not a regulator's processing promise.

| When | Task |
| --- | --- |
| Days 1 to 3 | Complete award details; locate certificates/transcripts; check BSc English medium |
| Week 1 | Request AIOU's historical syllabus and practical/exemption record; request science descriptors |
| Week 2 | Download demonstrably matching QQI statements; raise the B.Ed. profile query |
| Weeks 2 onward | Clarify professional-status documents and induction; prepare Biology evidence |
| When evidence is sufficient | Review the Council checklist, budget and application; submit |
| After submission | Respond to evidence requests; record decisions and deadlines privately |
| After registration | Apply using the exact registered status; negotiate hours separately |

Continue suitable office and education-support applications while recognition is pending. Do not promise teaching approval by arrival.
