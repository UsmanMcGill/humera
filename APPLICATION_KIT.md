# Application kit

Prepared 11 October 2026. These are editable templates, not a completed CV or verified references. Replace brackets with accurate information. No messages have been sent. Use [the guide](README.md) for role and permission rules.

## 1. Evidence checklist

| Evidence | Why collect it | Details to confirm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Degree certificates | Exact award identity | Awarding institution, name, dates |
| Full transcripts | Subject coverage and achievement | Credits/hours, levels, marks, practicals |
| B.Ed. programme handbook | Professional recognition | Education theory, subject methods, placement |
| Placement confirmation | Supervised teaching evidence | School level, duration, supervisor, assessment |
| English-medium letter | Potential accepted English evidence | Both instruction and examination |
| Employment letters | CV verification and references | Dates, role, subjects, level, hours, duties |
| Professional-status letter | Overseas teaching route | Correct competent authority and status |
| Thesis/research summary | Research support applications | Topic, actual methods and outputs |
| Two referees | Employer checks | Their consent and current contact details |

Never fabricate marks, job dates, publications, pupil outcomes or Irish equivalence. Documents and referee details belong in secure personal storage, not this repository.

## 2. Experience letter request

Suggested text for Humera to adapt and send to a former employer:

> I am preparing to apply for education and administrative roles in Ireland. Could you please provide an employment confirmation letter on official letterhead stating my job title, exact employment dates, subjects taught, student level, working hours and main responsibilities? Please include the signatory's name, position and a contact method for verification. I would also be grateful if you could confirm whether you are willing to act as a referee.

Suggested factual structure for the employer, who must verify and issue it:

> This is to confirm that [full name] worked at [institution] as [job title] from [date] to [date], for [hours] per week. Her responsibilities included [verified duties]. She taught Biology to [school grades/F.Sc. level]. [Optional verified assessment of performance.] This letter is issued by [name and position], who may be contacted at [official contact] for verification.

Do not add a claim that the letter establishes Irish teacher registration.

## 3. CV structure

Aim for about two pages, with plain headings and a readable layout. Submit the file format the employer requests. Use month/year dates once confirmed. A simple CV should contain:

1. Full name, professional email, phone and Cork location once known.
2. Three-line profile suited to the role.
3. Six relevant skills, each supported by experience.
4. Employment history, newest first, with three or four truthful duty/achievement bullets per role.
5. Education, newest first, with exact institutions and dates.
6. Relevant recent training and optional research/portfolio section.

A photograph, marital status, passport/PPS numbers and full immigration documents are unnecessary in the CV. Supply right-to-work evidence through the employer's legitimate process when requested.

### Profile for administration/support applications

> Education professional with a BSc honours and M.Phil. in Zoology, a B.Ed., and experience teaching Biology at school and F.Sc. level in Pakistan. Proficient in spoken and written English, with practical skills in Word, email, online meetings and basic Excel. Seeking part-time administrative or education support work in Cork.

Add organisation, records or scheduling skills only after she confirms actual examples. Do not portray teaching as a previous office administrator job.

### Profile for education/SNA applications

> Biology educator with school and F.Sc. teaching experience in Pakistan, a BSc honours and M.Phil. in Zoology, and a B.Ed. Interested in part-time school support and inclusive education roles in Cork. Proficient in spoken and written English and comfortable using digital tools for communication and teaching.

Do not say she is an experienced SNA or an Irish-registered teacher unless that becomes true.

### Useful bullets to complete from real examples

- Prepared and delivered Biology lessons for [level], covering [topics].
- Assessed [type of work] and provided [kind of feedback].
- Maintained [records she actually maintained] using [actual method/software].
- Communicated with [parents/colleagues/students] about [actual matters].
- Supported a learner who needed [type of support], by [actual actions].
- Completed M.Phil. research on [topic], using [actual methods].

Quantify only when she can substantiate the number. If there was a career break, use a short accurate explanation and focus on current readiness; do not invent work to fill it.

## 4. Availability and permission wording

**Before travel/permission approval:**

> Relocating to Cork in late January 2027. Expected to obtain employment permission as the spouse of a researcher on a Hosting Agreement; permission is not yet granted. Start date will depend on the applicable permission and onboarding requirements.

**Only after Stamp 1G is confirmed:**

> Authorised to work in Ireland under spouse Stamp 1G, without a separate employment permit. Evidence is available through the recruitment process.

**Schedule:**

> I am seeking approximately 20 to 30 hours per week, preferably mornings with a finish around noon or 2 pm. I can discuss occasional longer days in advance. School-term work would suit me particularly well.

Never substitute “available anytime” to get an interview. [Official Stamp 1G conditions](https://www.irishimmigration.ie/registering-your-immigration-permission/information-on-registering/immigration-permission-stamps/).

>

For university forms, address every essential criterion separately. A generic cover letter does not replace the required competency/application form.

## 6. Enquiries to prepare

### Teaching Council

Recipient: use the current contact on [the Teaching Council website](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/), rather than a guessed individual address.

> I am preparing to relocate to Cork in late January 2027. I hold a BSc honours in Zoology from [institution/year], an M.Phil. in Zoology from the University of Sargodha (2012), and a B.Ed. from [exact institution/year/programme length]. I taught Biology at high school and F.Sc. level in Pakistan for approximately [verified duration].
>
> Could you direct me to the appropriate overseas registration route and document guidance? I would particularly appreciate guidance on the competent-authority evidence required from Pakistan, supervised placement and induction evidence, the timebound provision, acceptable English-medium evidence, and the applicable assessment/registration fees. I understand that recognition requires a formal assessment and is not guaranteed by an enquiry.

### SNA course provider

> I am arriving in Cork in late January 2027 and have not completed QQI SNA Level 5. What is the next suitable Level 5 or combined Level 5/6 intake? Please confirm prerequisites, the exact QQI award, all fees, timetable, placement hours and arrangements, vetting requirements, funding eligibility and refund terms. My self-funded course budget cannot exceed EUR 1,000.

### Employer timetable/duties enquiry

> Could you confirm the contracted weekly hours, usual start and finish times and whether a morning pattern is possible? For this SNA role, could you also describe the personal care, mobility and behavioural support duties, and the training and staffing support provided?

Use this only for an actual advertised role and follow any instruction prohibiting enquiries. Enquiries are not job applications unless the advertisement says so.

## 7. Interview preparation

Prepare six examples using Situation, Task, Action and Result. Aim for a two-minute answer, with most time on what she did. Use real examples of:

1. Explaining a difficult Biology concept.
2. Supporting a student who was struggling.
3. Handling a difficult conversation respectfully.
4. Organising records or several competing tasks.
5. Learning a new digital tool.
6. Working with colleagues or correcting a mistake.

For administration, practise a short email, accurate data entry, calendar scheduling and an Excel sorting/filtering task with fictional records. For research support, practise explaining the thesis in two minutes and organising references. For an SNA interview, explain that the teacher directs teaching and that she would follow school safeguarding/care procedures, ask for training and raise concerns through the designated process. Do not claim untrained competence in lifting, medication or complex care.

If asked why her experience is overseas: describe it plainly, explain what transfers and show willingness to learn Irish systems. Do not disparage her university or say her degree is worthless.

## 8. Offer checklist

- [ ] Actual right-to-work evidence accepted; accurate start date.
- [ ] Employee rather than contractor status confirmed.
- [ ] Employer/location and 45-minute commute checked.
- [ ] Total workload, days and start/finish times agreed.
- [ ] Care or physical duties discussed and accepted where applicable.
- [ ] Gross pay, paid hours/breaks, holiday pay and deductions explained.
- [ ] Term-time closure and annual pay arrangements clear.
- [ ] Probation, notice, contract end and vetting conditions clear.
- [ ] Required training and who pays are clear.
- [ ] First-job Revenue registration and payroll contact identified.

Keep an offer unresolved until any material mismatch is answered. A lower salary does not make incompatible hours or duties suitable.
