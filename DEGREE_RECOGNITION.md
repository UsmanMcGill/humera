# Humera's degree recognition tutorial: BSc, M.Phil. and B.Ed.

Prepared and sources checked 11 October 2026. Start from Pakistan before the planned move to Cork in late January 2027. Recheck fees and requirements before submitting. This tutorial prepares applications; it is not an individual recognition decision.

## 1. Understand what you are applying for

There is no single Irish procedure that registers all three degrees and converts them into Irish qualifications. Use two processes:

| Qualification | Academic recognition | Professional teaching recognition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BSc honours in Zoology | Find an applicable QQI/NARIC comparability statement or request advice | Supply it with transcripts and subject evidence to the Teaching Council |
| M.Phil. Zoology, University of Sargodha, 2012 | Find an applicable statement or request advice | Include relevant subject/research evidence when requested |
| B.Ed. from an open university in Pakistan | Find an applicable statement or request advice | Have the Teaching Council assess the teacher education programme alongside the academic degrees |

NARIC's advice is free, generic and advisory. It compares an overseas award with an Irish National Framework of Qualifications (NFQ) award type or level. It does not authenticate Humera's certificates or grant a teaching licence. Employers, admissions offices and professional bodies make their own decisions. University reputation alone is not a reason to dismiss her qualifications. The awarding body's recognised status and the actual programme matter. [QQI explanation](https://www.qqi.ie/recognition-of-foreign-qualifications).

**Recommended sequence:** collect evidence, obtain academic comparisons for each award, then pursue teacher recognition if the evidence supports the route. Continue office and education-support job applications throughout. The B.Ed. should be assessed rather than assumed worthless.

## 3. Days 1 to 7: build the document pack

Keep personal documents in a secure private folder outside this GitHub repository. Suggested subfolders: `BSc`, `MPhil`, `BEd`, `TeachingCouncil`, and `RecognitionResults`. Use descriptive filenames such as `BSc_transcript_all_pages.pdf`. Scan all pages legibly, including reverse-side explanations of marks and credits. Retain originals.

For **each degree**, collect:

- Final degree certificate and complete official transcript/mark sheets.
- Programme dates, normal duration and admission requirements.
- Official module descriptions or a programme handbook for her cohort, if available.
- Evidence of the awarding body and campus if the teaching institution differs.
- Official translations where needed, kept together with the original-language documents.

For **BSc and M.Phil.**, also request science syllabi, laboratory/practical details, credit or contact-hour information, and the M.Phil. thesis title/abstract. Ask the university to explain its credit system. Do not convert Pakistani credits to ECTS yourself and present the result as official.

For **B.Ed.**, request a university letter explaining the teacher education programme, pupil age range, school placements, supervised teaching dates, hours/weeks, assessment and completion. A distance programme's placement evidence is especially useful; delivery mode alone does not establish its Irish acceptability.

For **employment**, request school/college letters giving precise dates, subjects, pupil ages, duties and whether the role followed professional qualification. Describe F.Sc. teaching as upper-secondary/intermediate teaching, not university lecturing. Employment letters support the record but do not replace a degree transcript or professional-status letter.

Suggested request text to adapt:

> I am preparing an overseas qualification assessment in Ireland. Please provide my complete transcript, programme/module descriptions for my study years, normal programme duration and admission requirements. For my teacher education qualification, please also confirm the supervised school placement and its assessment. Where accurate, please confirm separately that instruction and examinations were conducted in English.

Do not pay for blanket apostilles, HEC attestation, courier services or a commercial recognition agency simply because someone says they are always required. Follow the receiving body's current instructions and ask about an unclear requirement before spending.

## 4. Tutorial A: obtain academic advice for all three awards

### A1. Open and search the official database

1. Open the [QQI/NARIC foreign qualifications database](https://qsearch.qqi.ie/WebPart/Search?fref=gc&searchtype=recognitions).
2. Choose **Pakistan** under **Country**.
3. Choose **Higher Education** under **Type of Education**.
4. Select **Search**. Check that the results actually show Pakistan before using them. If the page keeps displaying other countries, reopen it from QQI's recognition page or use the advice form below.
5. Open a candidate qualification and inspect its title, duration, award profile, admission requirements, Irish award class and NFQ level.
6. Compare those details with the qualification record in section 2. A similar name is insufficient.
7. If applicable, open the **Comparability Statement** PDF link and save it in `RecognitionResults`. Record the database entry number, URL and download date.

The [official database guide](https://qsdocs.qqi.ie//Downloads/User%20Guide%20for%20NARIC%20database%20010217.pdf) illustrates searching, opening a qualification profile and downloading its statement. Button appearance may change.

### A2. Apply the checks separately

**BSc:** establish its exact duration and entry qualification first. Pakistani bachelor variants can differ. Do not assign NFQ Level 8 merely because the family describes it as honours. Save a statement only when its profile fits; otherwise request advice.

**M.Phil.:** QQI currently lists [Pakistan Master of Philosophy, entry 2294](https://qsearch.qqi.ie/WebPart/RecognitionDetails?recognitionCode=2294) as a two-year research programme comparable to a **Masters Degree, NFQ Level 9**. Its entry profile refers to four years of prior higher education in a related area, through a four-year bachelor or a master's following a bachelor. Check Humera's duration and admission sequence before relying on it. The statement is a useful candidate, not confirmation about her individual 2012 award.

**B.Ed.:** look for the exact award variant, duration and entry route. An academic comparison does not establish professional teaching eligibility. If no matching entry exists, use A3. The Teaching Council still assesses the programme and placement.

### A3. If there is no clear match, complete the advice form

Open [Request for Advice on Recognition of a Foreign Qualification](https://forms.qqi.ie/naric/award-queries). The current form has one qualification's details per request, so prepare a separate request for each unmatched award, with relevant previous qualifications attached or explained where useful. A listed, clearly matching award does not need a duplicate enquiry merely to obtain a named certificate.

Use her own contact email so she receives the reply. Work through the fields using her records:

| Form area | How to complete it |
| --- | --- |
| Personal/contact information | Her actual first name, last name and email |
| Qualification titles | Copy the original-language title and its accurate English title |
| Institution and awarding body | Distinguish the place of study from the body that issued the award |
| Country/institution status | Pakistan; give recognised status only if verified |
| Entry requirements and field | State the actual admission qualification and Zoology or Education |
| Duration, award year, research question | Use official dates/duration and answer the thesis question accurately |
| Qualification level | Give an established source-country level if known, not a guessed Irish level |
| Other details | Explain an unclear variant, historical programme or admission sequence concisely |

Attach the certificate, transcript and applicable official translations. The current form allows **up to 10 documents and 30 MB total**. It specifically says not to attach passports, marriage certificates, government licences, work permits or CVs. If a third party submits, its instructions require personal details in documents to be redacted; Humera applying herself avoids confusion about applicant identity. Review the privacy statement and all entries, then she can submit. Save the confirmation and watch email/spam for follow-up. QQI says the process can take several weeks. [Current advice form and upload instructions](https://forms.qqi.ie/naric/award-queries).

**Academic stage complete when:** each award has either a demonstrably applicable generic statement or a recorded advice request/result. An unanswered request remains pending.

## 5. Tutorial B: assess the B.Ed. for professional teaching

### B1. Confirm the route before paying

Based on her biology teaching to high-school/F.Sc. students, overseas **post-primary** recognition is the first route to investigate. It is a recommendation, not an eligibility finding. A B.Ed. intended for primary pupils may lead to a different assessment; do not select post-primary solely because she taught older pupils.

The standard overseas route requires a recognised teacher education qualification, relevant induction where required, official evidence of full professional qualification from the relevant authority, and at least one curricular subject. Ask the Council which Pakistani competent authority and evidence are appropriate if these are unclear. University completion and an employer reference are not automatically substitutes for professional status. [Overseas post-primary route](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/i-am-applying-to-register/qualified-outside-of-ireland/register-as-a-post-primary-teacher/).

If induction is missing, ask whether the current timebound provision applies. Do not interpret that provision as a waiver of degree, subject or vetting requirements. Use the enquiry draft in [the application kit](APPLICATION_KIT.md), adding the exact B.Ed. title, institution, duration and placement once confirmed.

Interest in special education does not by itself establish eligibility as a special education teacher. SNA employment is a separate pathway from teacher registration. Continue using the role distinctions in [the knowledge base](README.md).

### B2. Map Biology evidence

Open the [current curricular subject requirements](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/assets/uploads/2026/01/05.01.26-Curricular-Subject-Requirements.pdf), locate Biology and check whether a newer version is linked from the Council website before applying. Use its degree, subject-credit, study-level, content-area and practical requirements as the checklist. Zoology alone should not be assumed to cover every required Biology area.

Make an evidence table, adding one row per actual module:

| Module and qualification | Official credits/hours and year/level | Biology content covered | Laboratory/practical evidence | Transcript/descriptor page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| To complete | To complete | To complete | To complete | To complete |

Include relevant BSc and M.Phil. evidence and let the Council determine which study counts. Do not claim a thesis automatically replaces missing subject credits. Highlight specific pages so an assessor can find the evidence.

### B3. Prepare language, character and vetting evidence

**English:** proficiency is valuable, but formal evidence is required. First ask the BSc or B.Ed. university for a letter confirming both instruction and examination through English, if true. This is an accepted evidence route for a relevant qualification from a country where English is not the vernacular. If unavailable, check the alternatives before booking a test; the current list includes IELTS Academic at 7.0 or above and Cambridge C1 Advanced Grade C or higher, subject to the Council's conditions. [English requirements](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/i-am-applying-to-register/english-language-requirement/).

**Character:** follow the portal's reference and declarations instructions. If no suitable recent referee exists because of a career gap, request guidance rather than inventing a recent relationship. Keep employment history separate from supervised B.Ed. placement. [Application guidelines](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/assets/uploads/2024/01/Teaching-Council-Post-Primary-Guidelines-1.pdf).

**Overseas clearance:** record every country of residence since age 18. The Council requires national police clearance for the relevant overseas residence when the cumulative threshold of 12 months outside Ireland/Northern Ireland is met. Clearance must cover the residence period and be dated no more than six months before leaving that country, or any time after leaving. Check Pakistan's issuing process early and ask the Council if coverage is unclear. Registration cannot be finalised without required clearance. [Overseas police clearance](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/vetting/overseas-police-clearance/).

**Irish vetting:** follow the separate invitation process. Its current ID policy calls for one accepted identity document and one accepted address document, with address evidence dated within six months and matching the application. Use the actual policy list, not an assumed bank/mobile document. Do not upload these identity documents to QQI's academic enquiry form. [Vetting ID/address policy](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/vetting/guide-to-applying-for-vetting/teaching-council-id-policy/).

### B4. Enter and submit the application

1. Open the Council's overseas route page and follow its **My Registration** link.
2. Create her account using the appropriate applicant option and her actual current address. Do not claim residence in Cork before moving.
3. Select **Applications** and the route established in B1. Read the live checklist and current guidelines.
4. Enter personal, academic, teacher education, subject and teaching-service details from the factual record.
5. Upload the certificates, transcripts, descriptors, placement and professional evidence requested. Provide certified English/Irish translations where required.
6. Complete English, character, vetting and declarations steps as directed. Answer accurately, including gaps and missing evidence.
7. Save a draft if necessary, but set a reminder well before the three-month draft expiry.
8. Check the fee breakdown and total. Humera should review the declarations and payment herself before submitting.
9. Save the confirmation, reference and complete submitted pack privately. Track requests through the portal and email.

The current guidance allows three months to finish a draft and estimates up to 12 weeks for assessment after all required documents arrive. A draft, payment or acknowledgement is not approval. [Application route and timing](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/i-am-applying-to-register/qualified-outside-of-ireland/register-as-a-post-primary-teacher/).

### B5. Act on the decision

- **More evidence needed:** identify each item, request it from the correct institution/authority and respond with labelled documents.
- **Registration with conditions:** record every condition, deadline and permitted completion method. Ask about aptitude study or adaptation before enrolling. Confirm the Irish education-system requirement and any qualification shortfalls in her actual decision.
- **Refusal or disputed condition:** read the reasons and review instructions immediately. The published overseas guidance gives a 21-day review window and normally three years for conditions. Use her decision letter as the controlling instructions.
- **Registration finalised:** retain the registration details, check any remaining conditions and renewal date, and use the exact approved status in applications.

These are possible outcomes, not predictions. [Council assessment and conditions](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/i-am-applying-to-register/qualified-outside-of-ireland/register-as-a-post-primary-teacher/).

## 8. Describe the results accurately on a CV

Until assessed, list the actual qualification, university and year. Do not write “Irish registered degrees” or “Teaching Council registered” while an application is pending.

Where a generic statement genuinely applies, an appropriate description is: “QQI/NARIC generic comparability statement available for this award type.” Add the stated NFQ level only after checking its fit. If an employer needs individual verification, follow that employer's requirements.

For teaching, write “Teaching Council application submitted, decision pending” only after submission. Once registered, state the actual route/subject and applicable conditions accurately. Keep degree comparisons, teacher registration and immigration/work permission as separate facts.
