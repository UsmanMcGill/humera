## 2. Work permission and arrival administration

### Expected work route

An eligible spouse of a researcher on a Hosting Agreement may receive **Stamp 1G**, allowing employment without a separate employment permit and allowing study. It does **not** permit self-employment or operating a business. This spouse route differs from the graduate Stamp 1G scheme. Her chosen 20 to 30 hours is a family preference, not a student work-hours restriction. [ISD stamp conditions](https://www.irishimmigration.ie/registering-your-immigration-permission/information-on-registering/immigration-permission-stamps/).

A UCC contract or expected family permission is not itself proof that Humera can start work. Before a start date is agreed, obtain confirmation of her actual permission and acceptable evidence from UCC's immigration support/ISD and the employer. Ask specifically what proves her right to work between arrival and registration. Do not describe permission as already granted while the application is pending.

Visa-required eligible family members must still follow the Join Family entry-visa process. An exemption from an employment permit does not remove entry or residence requirements. Use current ISD guidance if older institutional pages describe earlier spouse-permit arrangements. [EURAXESS family guidance](https://www.euraxess.ie/ireland/fast-track-work-permit-non-eu-rd-hosting-agreement-scheme/researchers).

**Tutoring and remote work:** prefer an employment contract with an employer able to employ her in Ireland. A platform calling its tutors contractors, requiring invoices or paying as a freelancer is a reason to stop and clarify. Working online for an overseas company does not automatically resolve Irish immigration, payroll or tax requirements.

### Registration

Current ISD guidance directs first-time registration to Burgh Quay, Dublin, with appointments booked through the Customer Service Portal after arrival. A dependent sponsor must attend with the applicant. Budget for Dublin travel and the EUR 300 registration fee if applicable, separately from training. Check the current Stamp 1G checklist for both passports, sponsor's Hosting Agreement/permission, marriage evidence, address, relevant entry/landing evidence and insurance. Confirm exact documents before travel. [ISD required documents](https://www.irishimmigration.ie/registering-your-immigration-permission/how-to-register-your-immigration-permission-for-the-first-time/required-documents/).

### PPS number and payroll

She can look for work without a PPS number. A PPS application needs a valid reason, such as starting employment, and a basic MyGovID account. The current MyWelfare process includes an in-person appointment and subsequent checks; it is not instant. Prepare identity, address and reason evidence, and follow the generated document list. A PPS number is not a work permit. [MyWelfare PPS guidance](https://services.mywelfare.ie/en/topics/identity-services/personal-public-service-pps-number/).

For her first Irish job, register the employment in Revenue myAccount using the employer's Tax Registration Number. This enables the employer to receive the payroll notification; delay can produce Emergency Tax. Ask payroll for help and check the first payslip. Net pay depends on the household's tax position, so do not estimate it from gross wages alone. [Revenue first-job instructions](https://www.revenue.ie/en/jobs-and-pensions/starting-your-first-job/index.aspx).

## 3. Her qualifications and experience have potential value

University reputation, distance learning and an overseas teaching history are not automatic reasons to discard a qualification. Assessment depends on the actual award and evidence. Keep three decisions separate:

| Decision | Who decides | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Academic comparability | QQI/NARIC gives advisory comparisons | Search Pakistan and match the precise award and conditions |
| Professional teacher recognition | Teaching Council | Submit the required qualification/training evidence for the relevant route |
| Suitability for a vacancy | Employer | Match essential criteria and provide accurate examples/references |

QQI's database provides free, generic comparability statements. They are advisory, not individual accreditation or a teaching licence. If the precise qualification is missing, request academic recognition advice. Employers and professional authorities make their own recognition decisions. Do not put an Irish NFQ level on the CV until the matching statement supports it. [QQI recognition guidance and database entry point](https://www.qqi.ie/recognition-of-foreign-qualifications).

**Experience letters are worth collecting.** Request exact dates, job title, subjects, F.Sc./school level, hours, duties and a signatory who can verify the record. They can support employer references and an accurate CV. They do not replace a teaching qualification, registration or a competent-authority letter. Explain F.Sc. as Pakistan's upper-secondary/intermediate science programme; do not portray it as undergraduate university teaching.

## 5. School teaching and special education teaching

### Biology teaching assessment

Her B.Ed. should be investigated alongside the BSc and M.Phil. The overseas post-primary route assesses teacher education, professional status and subject eligibility individually. Gather official evidence of recognition from the competent authority in Pakistan and ask which authority/document is appropriate. Registration may carry conditions; an employer's experience letter is different evidence. [Overseas post-primary registration](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/i-am-applying-to-register/qualified-outside-of-ireland/register-as-a-post-primary-teacher/).

For Biology, current requirements include a qualifying degree comparable to NFQ Level 8, at least 180 ECTS overall or equivalent, at least 60 ECTS in Biology or equivalent, third-year-level study, laboratory/practical work and at least four specified subject areas. The areas include botany, plant anatomy/physiology, ecology, microbiology, zoology, mammalian anatomy/physiology, biochemistry and genetics. **Zoology alone does not establish the required breadth.** Prepare a module-by-module evidence table; let the Council decide equivalence rather than converting Pakistani credits yourself. [2026 subject requirements, Biology on page 8](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/assets/uploads/2026/01/05.01.26-Curricular-Subject-Requirements.pdf).

Gather B.Ed. module descriptions and supervised teaching placement details, including age group, duration and assessment. Clarify whether induction was required/completed. A timebound provision currently permits certain overseas-qualified teachers to complete induction in Ireland, with applications closing 31 December 2027. It does not waive qualification standards or guarantee her eligibility. [Teaching Council regulation announcement](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/news/the-teaching-council-introduces-revised-registration-regulations/).

English proficiency still needs accepted evidence. If a qualifying undergraduate/teacher education programme was taught and examined in English, obtain an official institution letter saying both. Do not pay for an English test before checking that evidence route. [English evidence options](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/i-am-applying-to-register/english-language-requirement/).

Current listed fees: EUR 200 initial qualification recognition, EUR 90 initial registration, EUR 100 extra post-primary subjects and EUR 65 annual renewal. Confirm which charges apply before payment. Allow up to 12 weeks after all documents arrive, with vetting and missing evidence potentially affecting timing. [Fees](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/i-am-applying-to-register/fees/) and [processing guidance](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/i-am-applying-to-register/application-processing-timeframes/).

### Special education teacher is a different route from SNA

An SNA supports care and access to education; a special education teacher teaches and requires the appropriate professional eligibility. A short SNA course does not qualify her as a teacher. Discuss the appropriate school sector and role with the Teaching Council before buying a programme. Primary registration introduces Irish-language requirements, so post-primary biology is the more direct route to investigate from her supplied experience. [Registration FAQs](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/i-am-applying-to-register/inital-registration-faqs/) and [primary Irish-language condition](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/assets/uploads/2024/05/Registration-Handbook-2024-2025.pdf).

UCC's funded postgraduate special education programme is for eligible serving teachers, with Teaching Council registration and relevant teaching duties. It is a potential later progression route after qualification and employment, not a free entry course for a new arrival. Check the current intake's conditions. [UCC Special Educational Needs diploma](https://www.ucc.ie/en/study/postgrad/taughtcourses/pgdip/specialed/).

Further Education is another registration route to investigate if a specific daytime vacancy fits. F.Sc. teaching does not automatically establish Irish FE eligibility. Cork ETB's self-financing tutor panel covers evening/night courses, which conflicts with her preferred schedule. [Teaching Council routes](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/i-am-applying-to-register/qualified-outside-of-ireland/) and [Cork ETB recruitment](https://www.corketb.ie/recruitment/).

## 6. Special needs assistance: a realistic but duty-dependent option

NCSE describes SNAs as supporting pupils with significant care needs. Duties can involve toileting, feeding, mobility or other care, rather than academic tutoring alone. Ask the school about the actual assignment, training, staffing support and physical requirements. Do not accept a role on the assumption that care duties can simply be removed. [NCSE SNA overview](https://www.ncse.ie/special-needs-assistant-sna/) and [Department SNA duties circular](https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-education/circulars/the-special-needs-assistant-sna-scheme-to-support-teachers-in-meeting-the-care-needs-of-some-children-with-special-educational-needs-arising-from-a-disability/).

The Department's minimum educational standard is not a universal requirement to buy a QQI SNA course: the published circular sets out three grade Ds in Junior Certificate, a grade C in Leaving Certificate, or equivalent. Ask the school how her overseas evidence meets equivalence and what additional requirements appear in its advertisement. A higher degree should not be treated as automatic confirmation of every entry criterion. [Educational qualifications circular](https://circulars.gov.ie/pdf/circular/education/2011/21.pdf) and [recruitment guidance](https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-education/services/special-needs-assistant-recruitment-and-qualifications/).

Look for part-time, fractional, job-share, fixed-term and substitute posts. Confirm actual arrival/departure times, paid hours, meetings, care duties and school holiday pay. A post fraction is not proof of a morning-only schedule. Historic Cork EducationPosts listings included part-time SNA posts at Cork ETNS and An Phairc NS with October 2026 deadlines; those are examples of role types, not open leads for January 2027. [Cork SNA search](https://www.educationposts.ie/posts/primary_level?cy=4&ga=0&p=1&sb=application_closing_date&sd=1&vc=13).

### Vetting and safeguarding

Garda vetting is arranged through a registered organisation, not purchased independently. The Teaching Council administers its own teacher vetting route. Prepare a complete address history and ask the relevant organisation what overseas clearance it needs. For initial teacher registration, the Council's overseas residence rules require police clearance in applicable cases; follow its current country-specific instructions. A Pakistani police certificate does not replace Garda vetting. [National Vetting Bureau FAQ](https://vetting.garda.ie/help/faq) and [Teaching Council vetting](https://www.teachingcouncil.ie/vetting/).

Complete Tusla's Introduction to Children First and retain the certificate. It takes approximately 1.5 hours and provides an Irish safeguarding introduction. It does not replace vetting or professional qualification. [Tusla course](https://www.tusla.ie/children-first/children-first-e-learning-programme/).

## 9. Hours, pay and offer checks

Ask about the finish time early. Examples to propose: 08:00 to 12:00 on five days; 09:00 to 14:00 on four or five days; 08:00 to 14:00 with the paid hours and break clarified. School start times may differ. A part-time teacher can have lessons scattered across a full day, with preparation, marking and meetings beyond contact hours. Count total workload.

The verified 2026 adult minimum wage is EUR 14.15 hourly for most employees. That is a dated reference, not a claimed 2027 rate. Recheck the legal floor before accepting. At an illustrative EUR 15 per hour, 20/25/30 paid weekly hours produce EUR 300/375/450 gross weekly, or approximately EUR 1,300/1,625/1,950 monthly over a full year (weekly pay multiplied by 52 and divided by 12). These are arithmetic examples, not salary forecasts. Term-time contracts can change annual income and payment patterns. [WRC minimum wage](https://www.workplacerelations.ie/en/what_you_should_know/hours-and-wages/national%20minimum%20wage/).

Part-time staff have employment protections and accrue leave. Breaks are not automatically paid: current guidance provides a 15-minute break after more than 4.5 working hours and 30 minutes after more than six, potentially including the first break. Ask how this affects finish times and paid hours. [WRC part-time guidance](https://www.workplacerelations.ie/en/what_you_should_know/employment_types/part-time-workers/part-time_workers.html).

Before accepting, confirm in writing: employee status, workplace, contracted hours, timetable, mandatory training/meetings, duties, hourly rate or pro-rated salary, holiday pay, school closures, probation, notice, contract end date, permission/vetting conditions and agreed flexibility. Do not accept unpaid employee trial shifts to prove herself; employment rights depend on the actual arrangement. [WRC unpaid work guidance](https://workplacerelations.ie/en/what_you_should_know/hours-and-wages/unpaid%20work/).
